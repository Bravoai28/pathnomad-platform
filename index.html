About

A fellow traveller on your creative journey

At our core, we are driven by a deep respect for the creative process and a commitment to empowering what we call Creative Nomads—artists, designers, and cultural practitioners who move fluidly across disciplines, geographies, and ideas. We understand that this path can be both inspiring and disorienting. That's why we offer strategic, compassionate support to help creatives organize their practice, refine their vision, and present their work to the world with clarity and confidence.

Whether you're an artist preparing for your first solo show, a designer developing a product line, or a brand seeking an authentic, artful collaboration, PATH NOMAD is your partner in shaping thoughtful, resonant experiences. We build bridges between creativity and structure, intuition and execution, vision and visibility.

PATH NOMAD is not just a service provider—we are a fellow traveler on your creative journey.

Frequently asked

Who can subscribe?

Practicing artists and creatives with a developed body of work. Applications are reviewed to maintain the curatorial standard of PATH NOMAD.

Why R350 per month?

The fee funds curatorial support, platform hosting, collector outreach, and member events — keeping the agency independent and accessible.

How do I access the member portal?

After your application is approved, you receive an access code by email. Sign in at pathnomad.co/portal with your email and code to manage your profile, view event archives, and membership details.

How does this relate to your agency work?

Membership extends our Creative Nomads philosophy from the company profile — the same team that curates exhibitions, experiential events, and brand development now supports your practice monthly.
